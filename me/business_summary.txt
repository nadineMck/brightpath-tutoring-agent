BrightPath Tutoring is a fictional tutoring center in Hamra, Beirut, with online lessons available worldwide. Its mission is to make difficult subjects understandable through patient explanations, guided practice, and study plans matched to each learner. BrightPath serves school students in Grades 6-12 and university students. Lessons are offered privately or in small groups of no more than four students.

The center teaches mathematics, physics, programming, and English. School mathematics includes algebra, geometry, trigonometry, pre-calculus, and exam preparation. University mathematics includes calculus, linear algebra, differential equations, probability, and introductory statistics. Physics covers school physics plus university mechanics, electricity and magnetism, waves, and introductory thermodynamics. Programming lessons cover Python, Java, C++, web fundamentals, debugging, and introductory data structures. English support includes grammar, conversation, reading, academic writing, presentation skills, and IELTS or TOEFL foundations. BrightPath does not currently teach chemistry, biology, medicine, or advanced graduate-level research topics.

The tutoring team consists of four fictional tutors. Maya Rahal teaches school and university mathematics and has eight years of tutoring experience. Karim Nassar teaches physics and holds a master's degree in applied physics. Lina Haddad is a software engineer who teaches Python, Java, C++, web fundamentals, and introductory data structures. Sarah Khoury teaches English language and academic writing and holds a CELTA qualification. Tutor availability varies, so the chatbot should describe normal schedules but never promise a specific tutor until staff confirm the booking.

Private school-level tutoring costs USD 28 per 60-minute lesson, while private university-level tutoring costs USD 35. Small-group school tutoring costs USD 16 per student per 60-minute lesson, and university small-group tutoring costs USD 20 per student. Groups contain two to four students at a similar level. A six-session small-group Programming Foundations package costs USD 110 per student. BrightPath offers a free 15-minute placement call, but it does not offer a free tutoring lesson. Payment is due before the lesson or package begins.

Regular teaching hours are Monday-Friday from 3:00 p.m. to 9:00 p.m. and Saturday from 9:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m., Beirut time. The center is closed on Sunday. Lessons may be online or in person at the fictional Hamra center. Students should request a preferred day and time, but all bookings require staff confirmation. Cancellations made at least 24 hours before a lesson can be rescheduled once without charge; later cancellations are charged in full except for documented emergencies.

BrightPath's unique value is a practical Learn-Practice-Reflect method: the tutor explains a concept in plain language, the student solves guided problems, and the session ends with a short progress note and next-step plan. BrightPath does not guarantee grades or exam results. For students under 18, a parent or guardian must confirm enrollment and payment. Prospective students should provide their name, email, subject, academic level, goals, preferred format, and schedule.
