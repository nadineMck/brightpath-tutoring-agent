# BrightPath submission video script

Target length: 4-6 minutes.

1. Show the repository structure and briefly open the business PDF and summary.
2. In `business_agent.ipynb`, show the separately labeled persona and instructions.
3. Show the three router labels and both documented tool schemas.
4. Start `python app.py` and open the Gradio link.
5. General question: ask about Grade 11 mathematics, prices, and schedules.
6. Course advice: describe a first-year engineering student struggling with mechanics.
7. Lead flow: ask to enroll, then provide a fictional name, email, level, and preference.
8. Point to `[TOOL] record_customer_interest` in the terminal.
9. Feedback flow: submit a fictional complaint or an unsupported question.
10. Point to `[TOOL] record_feedback` in the terminal.
11. Show `[REFLECTION]` and explain that both draft and revised replies are logged.
12. Run `pytest -q` and show all tests passing.
13. Close by showing `.gitignore` and confirming that `.env` is not uploaded.
